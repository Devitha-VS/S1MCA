holi

Holi is a major hindu festival of colours that celebrates spring,love,and triumph of good over evil.

Holi is asacred ancient tradition of hindus.It is a cultural celebration that gives hindus and non hindus alike an oppurtunity to have fun and play with other people by throwing coloured water and powder at each other.It is also observed broadly on the indian subcontnent.Holi is celebrated at the end of winter,on the last full moon day of the hindu luni-solar calender month,making the spring ,making the date vary with the lunar cycle .The date falls typically in march,but sometimes in late february of the grgorian calender.
